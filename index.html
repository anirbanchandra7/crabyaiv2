06FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Something we missed? Talk to the founders.

Why a crab?
Crabs carry their own shell, grow by molting, and never let go of what's theirs. That's the product: private by default, better with every molt, and fully yours. And yes, the only thing we're crabby about is your data.
When can I get access?
We're onboarding early-access teams in waves ahead of launch. Request access with your work email and we'll reach out with a start date and an onboarding call.
What does "own your intelligence" mean?
The fine-tuned version of your model is yours. Your data only ever trains your model, never anyone else's, and you can export your weights whenever you like. No lock-in.
How does Privacy Protect work?
Every prompt passes through a detection layer before it reaches the model. Sensitive values are replaced with typed placeholders, the model writes its answer, and Craby.AI restores the real values for authorised users only. The model never sees the originals.
How does the model improve?
You decide which conversations count as training data. Once enough are approved, Craby.AI fine-tunes a candidate, scores it on your evaluation set and promotes it only if it wins. Every version is kept, so rollback is one click.
Which models launch first?
We launch with two open-weight models and will roll out more over time. The endpoint is OpenAI-compatible, so moving to a new model later is a configuration change, not a rewrite.
